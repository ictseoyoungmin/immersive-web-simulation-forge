AWARD-GRADE WEB OPEN WORLD

NIGHT CITY, AT STREET LEVEL

Inspired by rain-drenched anime cyberpunk skylines: start as a human on the pavement, feel grounded weight, acceleration, friction, and jump arcs, then unlock flight as an option instead of the default. Activate the district relays and explore the alleys, plazas, over-lit towers, and flooded docks.

LOCOMOTION

Grounded by default. Smooth walking, sprinting, gravity, jump physics, collision against city blocks, and view bobbing. Press F only when you want free-flight.

WORLD

Procedural neon streets, megatowers, animated billboards, rain, fog, puddle reflections, relay objectives, landmarks, and a live minimap packed into a single offline HTML.

Desktop: mouse look + WASD. Mobile: on-screen movement controls appear automatically.

WebGL2 is required

This open world needs WebGL2. Please open it in a current Chrome, Edge, or Safari release with hardware acceleration enabled.
